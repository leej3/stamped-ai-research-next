## Result

**The analysis does not support a positive gain effect in vmPFC after whole-brain correction.** In the predefined vmPFC localization box, the mean participant effect was −0.0029 cope (95% CI −0.0225 to 0.0168). The box’s peak had *t*(53) = 3.92, but its whole-brain FWE *p* was .937. No voxel in the box survived correction.

A separate posterior/inferior result had 24 corrected positive voxels; its peak was *t*(53) = 6.62, FWE *p* = .0007. That finding is outside vmPFC and does not support the hypothesis. The whole-brain correction follows the [original NARPS instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf).
