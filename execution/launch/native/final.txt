**The analysis does not support a positive vmPFC gain effect under the whole-brain criterion.** The Harvard–Oxford Frontal Medial Cortex proxy had a mean effect of −0.009 upstream units (95% *t* interval [−0.040, 0.022]); no positive voxel in that region survived whole-brain Bonferroni correction. The proxy is an anatomical approximation, since vmPFC has no single agreed boundary. [NARPS Hypothesis 1](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf) predicts a positive effect; the [atlas label](https://git.fmrib.ox.ac.uk/fsl/data_atlases/-/blob/fsl-5_0_8/HarvardOxford-Cortical.xml) is “Frontal Medial Cortex.”

All 54 maps passed grid, finite-value, and checksum checks; none were excluded. Eleven positive voxels survived whole-brain correction, all outside the vmPFC proxy. This lack of corrected support does not establish that the vmPFC effect is zero. The estimates are in upstream contrast units, not change per currency unit.
