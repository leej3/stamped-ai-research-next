## Conclusion

This analysis does **not** support a positive gain effect in vmPFC. In the prespecified vmPFC sphere, the mean coefficient was −0.0027 (95% CI −0.0242 to 0.0187; one-sided sign-flip *p* = .607). No voxel there survived whole-brain correction.

The whole-brain test found 24 corrected positive voxels, peaking at *t*(53) = 6.62, FWE *p* = .0008 at MNI (−26, −102, −3.6)—outside vmPFC. That finding does not support the vmPFC hypothesis. The NARPS instructions call for whole-brain-corrected hypothesis decisions, which this analysis used ([original instructions](https://www.narps.info/files/narps_instructions_for_analysis_teams.pdf)).
